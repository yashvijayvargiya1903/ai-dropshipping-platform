# Automation Plan

## Principle
Automate repetitive operations while keeping human approval for money movement, legal/compliance decisions, irreversible supplier actions and ambiguous customer cases.

## Automation stages
1. Catalog normalization and pricing-rule calculation.
2. Address/order validation and order creation.
3. Authorized supplier fulfillment or owner action fallback.
4. Shipment/tracking monitoring.
5. Cancellation, return, exchange, RTO and refund workflows.
6. Database-grounded AI customer support with escalation.
7. Daily KPI, margin, RTO/return and unresolved-action reporting.

## Payment
Online payment is intentionally deferred. COD is the active checkout mode until the final payment phase.

## Supplier safety
Use only official/authorized integrations or user-assisted workflows. Never bypass CAPTCHA, anti-bot controls, access controls, or extract credentials/sessions.
