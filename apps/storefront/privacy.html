Privacy Policy

We collect the information needed to create accounts, process orders, provide support and deliver products. This can include name, contact details, shipping address, order information and payment status. Payment credentials are handled by the selected payment provider and are not stored by the storefront. We use Supabase for application data and authentication. Information is used only for operating the store, customer service, fraud/security controls, legal obligations and service improvement. We do not sell customer personal information. Customers may contact support to request access, correction or deletion where legally applicable.
