CUSTOMER CARE

Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Orders are usually dispatched within 1–2 business days. Delivery time depends on your location and courier service.

Returns & exchanges

You can request a return or exchange within 3 days of delivery. The product must be unused and returned in the same condition as received, with original packaging and tags.

Refunds & cancellations

You can cancel an order before it is dispatched. A full refund is available for approved cancellations and eligible returns after the product is received and checked.
