CUSTOMER CARE

Shipping, Returns & Exchange Policy

Shipping & dispatch

We aim to dispatch confirmed orders within 1–2 business days. Dispatch time is the time taken to hand an order over to the delivery partner; it is not the delivery time. Delivery timelines may vary depending on the destination, courier operations, weather and other circumstances outside our control. Once available, tracking details can be used to follow the shipment.

Returns & exchanges

Customers may request a return or exchange within 3 days from the date the order is delivered. To qualify, the product must be returned in the exact same condition in which it was received: unused, unworn, unwashed, undamaged and with its original packaging, tags, accessories and any items supplied with it.

Please raise the return or exchange request within this 3-day period and include your order number and a brief description of the issue. We may ask for photos to help verify the product's condition. Returns and exchanges are subject to inspection after the item is received. An exchange is subject to product availability.

Items that have been used, washed, altered, damaged after delivery, or returned incomplete may not qualify. Please keep the product and packaging safe until the request is resolved.

Refunds

Where a cancellation or return is approved and the product, if already delivered, is received in the exact same condition as sent, the customer is eligible for a full refund of the order amount. Refunds for prepaid orders will be initiated to the original payment method where supported. The time taken for the amount to appear depends on the payment provider or bank. For cash-on-delivery orders, our support team will communicate the available refund method.

Order cancellations

You may request cancellation before the order has been dispatched. If an order has already been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible; you can contact support to request a return within the applicable 3-day return window after delivery. A full refund is issued for an approved cancellation or eligible returned order, provided the return condition stated above is met where the product has been delivered.

How to request help

Contact Trendskartco support with your order number as soon as possible. Include photos where relevant and keep the product in its original condition until the request is reviewed.
