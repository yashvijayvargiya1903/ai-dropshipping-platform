CUSTOMER CARE

Policies

Shipping

Orders are processed after confirmation. Delivery estimates shown at checkout or tracking are indicative and depend on the carrier and destination.

Returns & exchanges

Eligible items may be returned or exchanged according to the product-specific return window and condition requirements shown with the order.

Refunds

Approved prepaid refunds are returned through the original payment method. COD refunds follow the refund method communicated by support.

Cancellation

Cancellation is available while the order is still in a cancellable processing stage. Once supplier fulfillment or shipment has started, manual review may be required.

Contact

For order-specific help, use the support flow and provide your order number.
