Refunds & Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before dispatch.

A full refund is available for approved cancellations and eligible returns. Return requests must be made within 3 days of delivery, and the product must be unused and in the same condition as received.

Approved prepaid refunds are sent to the original payment method where supported. COD refund details will be shared by our support team.

For help, contact Trendskartco support with your order number.
