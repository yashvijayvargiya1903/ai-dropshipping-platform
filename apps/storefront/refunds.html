Refund & Cancellation Policy

Order cancellation

Cancellation requests may be raised before an order is dispatched. Once an order has been handed over to the delivery partner, cancellation may not be possible. If the order has already been delivered, you may request a return within 3 days from delivery, subject to the return conditions below.

Refund eligibility

A full refund is available for an approved cancellation. If the order has already been delivered, a full refund is available when the returned product is received in the exact same condition in which it was sent: unused, unworn, unwashed, undamaged and complete with its original packaging, tags and accessories. Returned items are subject to inspection before approval.

Refund processing

For prepaid orders, approved refunds will be initiated to the original payment method where supported. The time for the funds to reflect depends on the payment provider or bank. For cash-on-delivery orders, our support team will communicate the applicable refund method.

Returns & exchanges

Return or exchange requests must be made within 3 days of delivery. Exchange requests depend on product availability and the item must meet the same condition requirements. Please provide your order number and photos if requested to help us review the request.

Contact

For order-specific support, contact Trendskartco support and provide your order number.
