Refund & Cancellation Policy

Cancellation requests are accepted while an order remains in a cancellable processing stage. After supplier fulfillment or shipment begins, a request may require manual review. Approved prepaid refunds are initiated through the original payment provider; the provider's processing time may apply. COD refunds, where applicable, are handled using the refund method communicated by support. Return or exchange eligibility depends on the product, condition and applicable return window shown with the order.

Contact

For order-specific support, use your account/order details when contacting Trendskartco support.
